7. SECURITY BOUNDARY AND YOUR RESPONSIBILITIES

The Software is a convenience tool for using WhatsApp Web. It is not a password manager, an
encrypted container, or a security product.

The Data folder holds your login sessions. Treat it as sensitive sign-in material: anyone
who can copy or open it may be able to access your accounts. The Software does not encrypt
the Data folder or your login sessions. You are responsible for protecting the Data folder
and the device it is on, for example with your Windows account security and disk encryption.

The PIN lock and the blur hide content on your screen from casual viewing. They do not
encrypt your data and do not protect the Data folder. Hiding the window from screenshots and
screen sharing depends on Windows and on the other program, and does not stop every way of
capturing the screen, such as some programs, other devices, or a camera.

Quick replies are stored in the Data folder encrypted with Windows data protection, so that
normally only your Windows user account on the same device can read them. This protection
depends on Windows and on the security of your Windows account, and is not a guarantee.
Anyone who can use your Windows account can read them, and quick replies that cannot be
decrypted, for example after the Data folder is moved to another device, are not available
there. Exported quick replies and settings files are encrypted only when you protect them
with a password; without one, anyone who gets the file can read it. A protected file is
only as safe as its password, and a forgotten password cannot be recovered by anyone,
including the developer. You are responsible for where you keep or send exported files. A
quick reply you pick is copied to the Windows clipboard, where other programs on your
device can read it.

You are responsible for the accounts you use with the Software and for the messages and
data in them, including personal data of other people, which you must handle in line with
the laws that apply to you. You are responsible for using WhatsApp in line with WhatsApp's
terms. The developer is not responsible for a restriction, suspension, or ban that WhatsApp
applies to your accounts.

Messages, links, files, and other content in your chats come from other people and from
WhatsApp, not from the developer. The Software opens web links in your default browser.
You alone decide whether to open a link, download a file, or open a downloaded file, and
you are responsible for doing so safely. The developer does not check, control, or endorse
that content or the websites it leads to.

Keep your own backups. The developer does not back up your data. Some actions you choose
